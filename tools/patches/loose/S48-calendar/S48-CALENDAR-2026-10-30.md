# S48 · Cleaner calendar on the four homes (30 Oct 2026)

Beau asked for a cleaner calendar: times were cut off, and picking start and end dates and times was fiddly.

## What changed (layer jpk-s48 v1.0.0, off switch `?s48=0`)
- **Month pills:** the time sits on its own line and is never cut. Weekends show it too (before, weekend cells hid the time). The title wraps to two lines and words aren't broken mid-word. Weekend columns are slightly wider (.85fr instead of .7fr).
- **New meeting form:**
  - **Date, Starts and Ends** are now buttons that open a picker when you click anywhere on them. Date shows "Fri, Oct 30, 2026" with a small month, ‹ › arrows, and Today, Tomorrow and Next Monday.
  - **Starts** is a list of times in 15-minute steps.
  - **Ends** lists only times after the start, each with its length (for example "2:15 PM · 15 min").
  - **Length chips:** 15 min, 30 min, 45 min, 1 hr, 1½ hr, 2 hr. The current length is highlighted.
  - **Meeting type** now runs full width, so "Client location" isn't cut.
  - **Keyboard:** Arrow Down opens a picker, the arrow keys move, Enter picks, and Esc closes the picker only (a second Esc closes the form).
  - **Unchanged:** the native inputs stay in the form, hidden, and still carry the values. Saving, the voice fill (S36/S37), the end-follows-start rule and All day work as before.

## Second pass (09:25, Beau's screenshot and "it goes away")
- **Day timeline on the home card:** the red now label ran 6px past the card's left edge, so "9:25 AM" read ":25 AM". The time column is now 66px (was 58px) and every hour label and the now label sit inside it.
- **Day range popover (the clock button, "7 AM to 6 PM"):** clicking the Start or End list closed the popover and quietly reset the range to 7 AM to 6 PM. The cause was S32's click check `closest('[data-s32]')`, which also matched `<html data-s32="on">`, so any click in the popover counted as the reset button. Now only the popover's own Save and reset buttons count (a one-line fix in the page, hash-guarded in the patch). The bug was live on all four homes.
